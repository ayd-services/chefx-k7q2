What the data shows

1. The run-up to Ramadan is one of the busiest stretches of the year

KIF published 68 events for 2026, with about 499 event-days across its halls. January and February had 9 new events each, as many as the busiest month of the year. 18 events overlapped the four weeks before Ramadan 2026 or Ramadan itself. Visitors, exhibitors and sponsors all have more choice in this window than at any other time.

Events starting each month at KIF, 2026 (Jan–Mar highlighted: run-up to Ramadan and Ramadan)

2. For Ramadan 2027 the calendar so far is all pre-Ramadan shopping

8 events are listed for the four weeks before Ramadan 2027 (expected around 8 February). Nothing is listed yet during Ramadan itself, between 8 February and 21 March 2027. Chef X's competition for visitors' evenings is mostly shopping fairs that end before Ramadan starts.

3. Food is rare at KIF, and nobody is teaching it

Only 2 of 68 events in 2026 were food-led: a hospitality trade show and a grocery and household fair. Across 225 archived KIF event pages from 2023–2026, 5 were food-led, and all were trade or shopping events. None was built around classes, chefs and tasting. In the published record, Chef X would be the only event of its kind.

KIF 2026 events by theme

4. Many organisers compete for the same exhibitors

The archived pages name more than 60 different organising companies. KIF itself organised 59 of the 225 events, so about 74% are run by private organisers like Spot Global. Restaurants, cafés and food brands get offers from many of them. The organiser who can show results gets the booking.

5. Almost nobody publishes results

Only 5 of 225 event pages give any figure for visitors, exhibitors or floor size. Sponsors are asked to pay KD 5,000–15,000 with little evidence to compare against. An organiser that reports real numbers after the event stands out immediately.

What it means for Chef X

  • Position Chef X as the one learning-and-tasting event of Ramadan. The calendar has many shopping fairs and none built on classes.
  • Sell sponsors on proof. Promise each sponsor a results report: footfall, class attendance, social reach and leads. Hardly anyone else gives one.
  • Sell booths to the right brands. Food and café brands that already exhibit at the pre-Ramadan fairs are the warmest leads for Chef X's booths.
  • Track every lead and booking in one place, so next year's sales start from data, not from a phone contact list.
