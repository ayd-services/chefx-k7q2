1. Money: did the ads pay back?

KD 5,250 went on six paid campaigns. They brought 1,060 bookings, so each booking cost KD 4.95. At an average ticket of KD 15, that is KD 15,900 in ticket revenue, or 3.0× the spend, before sponsor value. Another 640 bookings came with no ad: from posts, shares and word of mouth.

Bookings per week (the week before opening was the peak)

2. The journey from ad to seat

The ads were seen 1,960,000 times. Of 26,950 clicks, 19,134 reached the Chef X website (71%) and 1,913 started checkout. 853 people started checkout and did not finish. That is the cheapest place to win bookings: a reminder message and a simpler payment step. 1,496 of 1,700 booked seats (88%) were actually used.

From ad to booking

3. The Chef X website

The website had 31,384 visits during the campaign. Ads sent 61% of them, but people arriving from WhatsApp shares booked most often: one booking for every 15 visits, against one for every 18 from ads. Friends sharing the link work better than any ad, so the site should make sharing one tap.

6. Posts without ads

Chef videos did the most work: 24 reels reached 18,400 people each on average, three times more than recipe carousels, and brought 2,310 taps on the booking link. Stories were posted most often (140) but each one reached fewer people.

7. What people say

We read every comment and message, in Arabic and English, and grouped them by topic. Almost a third ask about prices. The answer should be pinned and in the first story frame. "Booking problems" (7%) needs fixing before the next campaign.

Comments and messages by topic (%)

8. Are enquiries answered?

1,240 enquiries arrived across Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp and email. The median reply took 3 h 40 min, 22% waited more than a day, and 96 were never answered. People answered within an hour booked 2.3× as often as those answered the next day. A shared inbox with alerts fixes most of this.

11. Five actions for the next campaign

  1. Move budget from the Snapchat teaser and paid influencer into retargeting and chef reels.
  2. Send a WhatsApp reminder to everyone who started checkout and stopped. That is 853 people.
  3. Answer every enquiry within one hour during campaign weeks. Use one shared inbox and set an owner per shift.
  4. Post chef videos after iftar on Wednesday and Thursday. Put prices in the first story frame and in a pinned post.
  5. Catch up the missing sponsor stories during the event, and track every promise daily. Make the website link easy to share on WhatsApp.
