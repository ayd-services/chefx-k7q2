What it does

Everyone in one place

Restaurants, cafés, home businesses, online sellers, bloggers, influencers and sponsors, each with a type, contact person, owner and full history.

Who brought whom

Every sponsor and exhibitor is linked to the person or referral partner who brought them, with the commission agreed and paid.

Spend that pays back

Every fee paid to an influencer next to what they promised, what they delivered and the bookings their code brought.

The red list

Everyone who said no, with the reason and a date to try again, so no contact is wasted next year.

Messages from every channel attach to the right contact, so the history is never lost in one person's phone.

Yours, in Arabic and English
